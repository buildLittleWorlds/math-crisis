## Suggested reading order

Start with the recording and its [quote audit](quote-check.md), then read the declaration, Gowers, Cohn, and Antieau. Cowen and Gans make the external public-interest challenge sharper; Kra and Loh explain different reasons for sustaining human expertise. The SAIR announcement shows what one constructive response proposes to build.

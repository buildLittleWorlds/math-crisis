# Illustration set

Created September 21, 2026 with built-in Imagegen. All three originals were 1536 × 1024 PNGs. The website uses quality-84 JPEG copies for smaller downloads. The two later images used the original as a style reference. All images were visually inspected; these are fanciful editorial illustrations, not depictions of actual AI systems or mathematicians.

## First image prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: polished website feature illustration for an adult AI explainer publication, "AI, Explained Over Coffee"; illustration only, not a website mockup.
Primary request: A whimsical, friendly abstract idea machine / playful retro computer offering a floating puzzle piece, expressing mathematical discovery and curiosity.
Scene/backdrop: A warm butter-yellow paper background; a coffee cup beside the machine, with a small number of floating geometric puzzle pieces and curious orbital shapes.
Subject: One charming cobalt and ink-blue retro computer/idea machine with a simple friendly face and expressive curved arm offering a vermilion puzzle piece. Confident simple silhouettes, sophisticated and approachable for adults.
Style/medium: Mid-century editorial screenprint illustration; warm cartoony shapes, subtly imperfect ink edges and restrained paper texture.
Composition/framing: Landscape 3:2. Balanced, self-contained composition with comfortable space around the subject, suitable for a yellow feature-story panel. Strong readable silhouette even as a modest-size website image.
Lighting/mood: Cheerful, curious, relaxed, clever.
Color palette: Cobalt blue, deep ink blue, butter yellow, vermilion orange, warm cream; tight palette with rich contrast.
Materials/textures: Subtle screenprinted paper texture, flat shapes, no glossy 3D rendering.
Text: No lettering or text.
Constraints: One finished illustration. No logos, no watermark, no mock webpage, no scary robot, no neon, no cyberpunk, no childish clipart.
```

## Article prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: standalone dedicated math-discovery article illustration for an adult AI explainer publication.
Input images: Image 1 is a STYLE REFERENCE only. Match its warm mid-century screenprint texture, cobalt and ink blues, orange, yellow and cream, charming retro technology and confident shapes. Create a distinct new composition.
Primary request: A friendly retro blue idea machine discovers mathematical connections by arranging geometric puzzle pieces beneath a playful balancing mobile.
Scene/backdrop: Butter-yellow paper background. Delicate mobile arms balance a circle, triangle, cube and puzzle piece. Clear intentional mathematical symbols float in open space around the discovery.
Subject: One friendly blue retro idea machine, delighted and curious, arranging two contrasting geometric pieces so they fit together; pose and framing distinct from the reference image.
Style/medium: Sophisticated warm cartoony mid-century screenprint editorial illustration for adults. Strong simple silhouettes, subtle paper grain and imperfect ink edges.
Composition/framing: Landscape 3:2, balanced self-contained composition with breathing room around every symbol and comfortable edge space.
Color palette: Cobalt blue, deep ink blue, butter yellow, vermilion orange, warm cream.
Text: Only these five clearly drawn standalone mathematical symbols: + = × π ∞. Each symbol clean and recognizable; no equations or words.
Constraints: No words, lettering other than intentional pi symbol, logos, watermark, mock website, scary robot, neon, cyberpunk or glossy 3D. Mathematical symbols should be clear and distinct, not decorative gibberish.
```
